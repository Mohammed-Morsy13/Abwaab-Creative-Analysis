📊 Creative Performance Report

Meta Ads Creative Performance

KSA Market · January – April 2026

🗓 Period: Jan 1 – Apr 30, 2026 📍 Market: Saudi Arabia 🎯 Objectives: Messaging · Form Leads · Signups 🧠 Vertical: EdTech
📌 Executive Summary
The numbers behind the four months — what worked, what didn't, what to do next

The Headline

Across the four months, the CT account spent $37,588 across 8 CT campaigns and generated 16,026 conversions (messaging conversations + form leads + signups) at a blended cost of $2.27 per conversion, plus 8,228 engagement interactions from the Engagement campaign that fed the WhatsApp inbox, plus 11,722 link clicks from the Traffic campaign. Two creatives alone — Flex-Videos-Qudoraat (1,816 leads at $0.51) and Monsif – Taasis Post (1,671 conversations at $0.99) — produced 21.7% of all conversions in the entire portfolio.

The Top 10 winners produced ~76% of all conversions on ~58% of total spend. The Bottom 10 losers consumed ~9% of total spend for ~3% of conversions. Reallocating from losers to winners is the single largest improvement opportunity heading into May.

What This Report Covers

Top 10 WinnersThe creatives that produced the most results, most efficiently, with enough spend to prove the performance is real — not a small-sample fluke.
Bottom 10 LosersThe creatives that wasted the most money — either by spending heavily at high cost-per-result, or by producing zero results despite meaningful spend.
Strategic Action PlanConcrete decisions for May 2026 — what to scale, what to pause, what to retest, and the budget thresholds that should trigger automatic action.
